## tea / mochi / package (Codex call 1)

Prompt (abridged only in the shared style preamble; subject text verbatim):
> view_image the four style references ... cozy handcrafted chunky pixel art, warm, soft, slightly silly ... final sprite is only 32x32 art pixels, so design with very simple bold shapes ... soft light from top-left, selective dark outline in ink #1D1F33 ... [CGE palette] ... THREE separate images, each ONE subject centred with generous margin on a SOLID FLAT MAGENTA #FF00FF background, no text, no drop shadow, no steam:
> (1) a small round chubby teapot with a little teacup beside it, teal and sage glazed ceramic with a small amber lid knob and a cream highlight, square-ish composition, viewed slightly from above-front.
> (2) a small cream ceramic plate seen from a 3/4 top view holding three round moon mochi balls (one pale pink, one white, one light green), soft and squishy with tiny highlights, wide composition (about 4:3).
> (3) a furoshiki-wrapped delivery bundle: a plump square parcel wrapped in terracotta cloth with a subtle cream dotted or wave pattern, a big bunny-ear knot on top, and a tiny cream paper tag hanging from the knot, square composition.

Normalization (final):
```
python tools/art/pixelize.py art-src/items/raw/tea-v1.png     public/assets/items/tea.png     --size 32x32 --colors 28 --anchor bottom --outline 1D1F33 --pad 1
python tools/art/pixelize.py art-src/items/raw/mochi-v1.png   public/assets/items/mochi.png   --size 32x24 --colors 28 --anchor bottom --outline 1D1F33 --pad 1
python tools/art/pixelize.py art-src/items/raw/package-v1.png public/assets/items/package.png --size 32x32 --colors 28 --anchor bottom --outline 1D1F33 --pad 1
```
(Actually written to `art-src/items/work/<name>-o.png` then copied to `public/assets/items/<name>.png`.)

Iteration notes: first normalization without `--outline` lost the raw ink outline (≈0.4 art px after the ~39x downscale) and the silhouettes faded on dark space; re-run with a 1px ink #1D1F33 outline + 1px pad matches the hero ship's outline weight. No manual pixel edits. One generation attempt each was sufficient.

## steam (programmatic)

`python art-src/items/scripts/make_steam.py public/assets/items/steam.png`

Authored directly at art resolution: identical puffs travel up a fixed S-curve, spacing (4.8 px) equals the 4-frame travel, so frame 4 -> frame 1 loops seamlessly. Alpha quantized to 4 steps (70/130/190/235) for crisp pixel edges with partial transparency; plaster #FBF7EC core, cool #B4C5CE rim on the lower-right (light from top-left). Iterations: separate dots (too sparse) -> smooth ribbon (motion invisible) -> current middle ground with visible travelling bumps. Not generated by Codex: a 16x24 soft-alpha wisp is cleaner authored procedurally than downscaled from a 1k image.

## memory-postcard (Codex calls 2 and 3)

v1 prompt: illustrated pixel-art postcard, 3:2, parchment border, dusk-blue/plum sky with twinkle stars, large pale jade Tea Moon on the left with a tiny tea house (amber window, terracotta roof) on top, the gyoza ship flying right with a puff trail, scalloped stamp top-right with a terracotta teacup motif, no text. Result was charming but the tea house and ship collapsed to a few pinkish pixels at 90x58 and the raw border became ~1.5 art px.

v2 prompt (revision of v1, which Codex viewed): NO border (full-bleed sky); tea house ~3x bigger (cream walls, big curved terracotta roof, one large glowing amber window, ink outline, one round tree, no fences/lanterns); ship bigger and matching the reference (dough-cream body, toasted golden pleats, glass dome, puffy cream trail); stamp bigger with one bold terracotta teacup; fewer, larger clouds; no text.

Normalization (final):
```
python art-src/items/scripts/make_postcard.py art-src/items/raw/postcard-v2.png public/assets/items/memory-postcard.png 40 0,0,1536,1024
```
The script crops the picture box, runs `pixelize.py <crop> <tmp> --size 90x58 --fit cover --opaque --key none --no-crop --colors 40`, then frames it in a hand-drawn 3px parchment border (1px #D8C49E card edge, #ECDFC5 lower/right bevel, #F4ECDC fill, 1px transparent rounded corners). 40 colours chosen over 32 to keep the window glow and stamp legible.
